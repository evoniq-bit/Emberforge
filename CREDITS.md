# Emberforge Credits

## Turk645 – Builder's Companion

Special thanks to **Turk645**, the original creator of **Builder's Companion** for Enshrouded.

The original concept and functionality behind overriding normally placeable building blocks and objects with materials and objects from the game world came from his work.

Builder's Companion was also an important inspiration for other ideas and functionality in Emberforge. Some of those features were rebuilt from the ground up, adapted to current versions of Enshrouded, expanded with additional options, or integrated into Emberforge in a different form.

For Emberforge, the Building Block Override and Object Override systems in particular have been adapted, rebuilt and expanded into a standalone application with additional functionality such as:

- Material capture
- Material ID override
- Material favorites
- Object capture
- Configurable source objects
- Object catalog
- Object favorites
- Nearby object scanning
- Configurable hotkeys
- Additional building, inventory, crafting and gameplay tools

Even where Emberforge now uses a different implementation or significantly expanded functionality, the influence of Turk645's work should be properly acknowledged.

Without Turk645's original work and the ideas demonstrated in Builder's Companion, several parts of Emberforge probably would not exist in their current form.

Thank you for making your work available to the Enshrouded community.

**Original project:** [Builder's Companion by Turk645 on Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/enshrouded/mods/32)
